# Who moved money on five chains in two minutes

5 September 2026, 09:31:23 to 09:33:23 UTC. Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon. Source: every user transaction in which one address changed one asset position by at least $10,000, ranked by `scripts/amount_outliers.py` at prices pinned to the sample end (ETH $2,458, BTC $79,706, POL $0.094, ARB $0.13, OP $0.10), and the qualitative notes in `qual_notes.md`.

Dear reader,

Ask which transactions moved more than $10,000 and the five chains answer with five different stories. Ethereum had 82 such transactions, Base 661, Optimism 292, Arbitrum 17 and Polygon 8. The counts say less than they seem to, because on the two OP-stack chains almost all of the "large" transactions are the same few robots doing the same thing every block.

**Base and Optimism: liquidity that exists only between blocks.** On Base a helper contract [minted a $1.9M USDC/WETH position](https://basescan.org/tx/0xdd9809d45d3ceadc036f1ab21380dc899eea3e2229e271eca65bea04542359e2) as the last transaction of a block, staked it in the Aerodrome gauge, and [burned it](https://basescan.org/tx/0x38b025b2b832211856bc40afaffb410dfa55e493ac2cdba54c6b575d4b4ff960) as the sixth transaction of the next, 57 times in two minutes; over the window it netted minus $1,313 on $117M passing through. A second operator [cycled $1.2M](https://basescan.org/tx/0x48627df7953c53400eac59dc3532886168a7d97956a51d1440e917927a10eea1) in the same pool and [$211k](https://basescan.org/tx/0x7dfab7528beef88b1cd8dc53c0c35e3b8506dd15ddfa8d61a95a8045eff4bf8d) in the cbBTC pool through three rotating wallets. On Optimism three systems did the same to one Velodrome pool, one of them moving exactly 30,000 USDC twice in every block. The reason is time: gauge emissions accrue by elapsed seconds, and seconds only elapse between blocks, so a position that sits in the pool across the boundary and leaves during the block earns the rewards without ever being there when someone swaps. The pilot had already saved 30 minutes of history for these pools, and a check confirms it: in the Optimism pool the bots burned at a median block position of 3 and re-minted at 25, and only 3 of 40 swaps met their liquidity; in the Base pool, 4 of 183. Read the pool's liquidity at the end of a block and you will see money that no trade can touch.

**Ethereum: stablecoins in, stablecoins out.** The largest movements were a [4,000,000 USDT repayment](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x61d078ac534d6dcd296d556248f1a54d58ba1d1f6aaac94c7a8654d71a78015e) of an Aave borrow, a [3,000,000 USDT deposit](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xdf8ec771f632686012e3d717c0b3b294fa63356e5afb126c301c2fd9e988bebf) into a hot wallet that took in $3.1M of USDT and $84k of LINK while sending 20 transactions, [999,985 USDT](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x07ee2cd4a8b935d56d7f422fd9ad02557e87468889c2f90c029c83868e748b82) from a wallet on its second transaction, and [786,000 USDS turned into USDC at par](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x1db694feffa59e7101e99e3e37d233220ef9b727a948875e1af44a21756976a1) through the Sky PSM for a fee of four cents. Every large ETH transfer was exchange plumbing: withdrawals from wallets with nonces of [793,355](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xcee708511d08852f7c5489ad09793644921e67db31e9586b63ff3123e9a99487) and [15,190,919](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x03c508303a341ca53ee7905d48d63f1ee0eaf3f2238a5b2e9e6cc5ebbc2d4ad7), and a [57 ETH wrap](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x6b0a5908e135ce92133765b553b39346a9c1360763c56a9cd3a13eadfe4845e1) by a trading wallet. Two Morpho flash loans of [9,826 WETH](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x5b6b354951c145149d346fae74c33d3377aa17ff3ccc584024457c9236aaffd7) and [1,000 WETH](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x3a9fdfc182bdb7a6f1ba6925666ed1d80f193b698c117a9296d5987ea648c53e), $24M and $2.5M, backed arbitrages whose trading legs were a few hundred dollars each, the second clearing about $41; the lender's WETH netted exactly zero on $53M through. Only gross volume notices them, and gross volume measures bot configuration, not money.

**Arbitrum and Polygon: deposits and consolidation.** [2.04 million ARB](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x5b8bb73ee21405ba5ae1f5ca723e76e89e0e89021950a5b671b83cd40d582cbf), worth $270k, went into a wallet that sent 26 transactions in the window; two wallets [emptied 20.6](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x86d9cf169d5c660e41f2c88e61649f13e36f4faa46536452ee492b5b09416076) and 15.3 ETH into one address in one block; an operator [paid 38,663 USDC to 51 vault clones](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x742fb6b55e7b2bafed1d2544ee72e411140423905a52f16e59647deea9886d46) in one transaction; and 200,000 USDT landed on an [EIP-7702 delegated account](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x4b3b5eb9efd3d683892c1213ae09fee6b117a2dff6655feadf972b69bb1205e6). On Polygon the biggest native transfer, [198,010 POL](https://polygonscan.com/tx/0x48f8502d6f5ea6fef2bcbf5ba0d3796ad3ceb66118b2c14c2124484e2d591a20), is $18,699, and it went to the same address as [89,980 USDT](https://polygonscan.com/tx/0x4723c1d868db4e782593975257a9edc805230c4ff77bd3d5ea91e579140f689c). A 209-byte deposit forwarder received USDC at the same address on [Arbitrum](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x8f56222667941e41843d418769ea155545204cc559c13d76929dc710f6fd74b1) and on [Polygon](https://polygonscan.com/tx/0x2b214f60d1ef3d16918e8340dd5ea9b767f315b4e28e4365a9e16f360a72000a). Two users sold USDT for DAI through the same [aggregator](https://polygonscan.com/tx/0x3ce3501eba4ee3cf15f2116e2905775dc86ec501170ed8d9ace91cef80a2b57e) a minute apart, each trade split into a dozen or more legs, so that $6.8k of USDT registered as $68k of gross volume.

**A note on fakes.** The most transferred tokens the scan could not price were, on Ethereum, '꒤SDT', a second 'USDT' at another address and '꒤SDC', and on Arbitrum 'ÚSDС' with a Cyrillic letter: address-poisoning spam. Pricing by symbol would have counted them as dollars. Pricing by address ignored them.

**What to take from it.** A dollar threshold finds three things: real flow (exchange deposits and withdrawals, a treasury repaying a loan, a user changing stablecoins), routed or borrowed value that inflates gross numbers without moving anyone's position, and machines that cycle capital through pools every block to farm emissions. Netting each address over the window separates the three, and it should run before anything is called a large transaction. Contract names given without an onchain check are hypotheses, and two minutes is a glimpse.
